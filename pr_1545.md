## What does this PR do?
<!-- Please include a summary of the changes and the related issue. Please also include relevant motivation and context. -->
This PR implements full hashtag support for the NexaSphere forum. It allows users to use `#hashtags` in their threads and replies, automatically parses them into clickable links, tracks their usage for a trending list, and enables users to follow their favorite hashtags.

Fixes #1545
